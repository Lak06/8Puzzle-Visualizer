How to read this

  • Cutoff means the limit was too small. Some branches were stopped at depth l and a deeper solution may exist.
  • Failure means every state within reach was searched and the goal is not among them.
  • The first limit that finds a solution is at least the optimal depth. A larger limit may return a longer path than needed, because DLS is not optimal.
  • Work grows quickly with the limit, roughly bl without repeated-state checking.

Only Depth Limited Search is implemented on this site, as assigned for this registration number, so the table compares DLS with itself across limits instead of against other algorithms.
